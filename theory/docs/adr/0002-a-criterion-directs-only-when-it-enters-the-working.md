# Intelligence is the working of an organised system under a criterion that enters the working

The vault models intelligence as "the context-dependent capacity of an organized system of transformations to produce effective … state transitions under task criteria, goals, or value gradients", and says direction "can be supplied by the task, environment, or evaluator" without being an internal goal. The first Core let a criterion attributed to a system suffice, so any organised system realised intelligence under any criterion written next to it; a JPEG pipeline with `criterion(pipeline, integrity)` counted although nothing in it responded to integrity. The Core now requires the criterion to enter the working (`directed_by/2`): from inside, a component takes the criterion up (`directed_when_component_takes_up_criterion`); from outside, an evaluator judging under it takes the system's output up and its response returns into the system (`directed_when_evaluator_returns`). Intelligence is an organised system so directed (`intelligence_when_organised_and_directed`); it is a working, not a capacity the system owns, and a criterion is not an obligation (hence "criterion", not "task"). Decided at the rendering gate of issue #2 (PR #10).

## Considered options

- Attribution suffices (the first Core). Rejected: the intelligence Claim then adds nothing to the organisation Claim; a label makes anything intelligent.
- Require an internal representation of the criterion only. Rejected: the vault says direction can come from the environment or an evaluator; natural selection, a market and a reviewer supply direction without handing the system its criterion. The evaluator route keeps "intelligence and agency are distinct" true.
- Add generativity to the definition. Rejected: the reading map keeps generativity in the value branch, separate from the mechanistic branch, and making intelligence require it would turn generativity into a measure of intelligence, against "humans are not an optimization target".
- Require recurrence. Rejected: the vault does not assert recurrence as a necessary condition; recurrence stays a separate Claim, and revision through it is `provisional`.

## Consequences

- A criterion that is only attributed directs nothing (`pipeline_with_an_attributed_criterion`); an evaluator that judges and takes the output up but never replies directs nothing (`team_judged_without_reply`).
- The Core does not grade intelligence; a two-component pipeline with a comparator that takes the criterion up realises intelligence under it. The vault's grading is not formalised (issue #5).
- Revision of the criterion itself through the working (adaptivity) is left to the desire-as-recursive-value-formation slice.
